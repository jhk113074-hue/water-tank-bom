Panel Cost Calculation Logic & Formulas

×

1. Raw Material Cost (원자재 비용)

Raw Material Cost = (Panel Weight × SMC Rate) + Sub-Material Cost ($)
* SMC Rate default: $5.00 / kg | Sub-Material Cost default: $1.32

2. Fabrication Cost (가공비)

Press Hours = Press Sec / 3600
Press Cost = (Press Hours × Press Machine Rate) + (Press Hours × 2 Persons × Direct Labor Rate)

Drill Hours = Drill Sec / 3600
Drill Cost = (Drill Hours × Drill Machine Rate) + (Drill Hours × 2 Persons × Direct Labor Rate)

Fabrication Cost ($) = Press Cost + Drill Cost
* Press Rate default: ~$34.60/hr | Drill Rate default: ~$7.89/hr | Labor Rate default: ~$7.22/hr

3. Single Panel Cost (단판 가격)

Single Panel Cost ($) = Raw Material Cost + Fabrication Cost
* Manual single price overrides take precedence when provided.

4. Insulation Panel Cost (단열판 가격: 25mm / 40mm)

Insulation 25mm Mat = (Skin × Skin Rate) + (MDI × MDI Rate) + (Polyol × Polyol Rate)
Insulation 25mm Total ($) = Single Panel Cost + Insulation 25mm Mat + Insulation Labor ($3.50)

Insulation 40mm Mat = (Skin × Skin Rate) + (MDI × 1.6 × MDI Rate) + (Polyol × 1.6 × Polyol Rate)
Insulation 40mm Total ($) = Single Panel Cost + Insulation 40mm Mat + (Insulation Labor × 1.25)
